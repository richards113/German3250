Meet Your Instructor

"Welcome to German 3250: Citizenship in the Age of Technology! I am Professor Kevin Richards. Course Themes & Structure

Three focused 5 week sessions exploring the intersection of human rights, identity, and algorithmic governance.

Part I

Citizenship

Examine the public sphere in an automated world. Topics include social media dynamics, autonomous drone warfare, labor automation, and digital escapism.

Portfolio I:

Defining Digital Citizenship in an Automated Age

Part II

Diversity & Freedom

Investigate virtual identities, the coding of gender in databases, human AI relationships, and the role of digital activism in smart cities.

Portfolio II:

Freedom, Diversity, and Digital Identity

Part III

Justice

Analyze systemic structures: dataveillance, algorithmic bias in courts, crypto economics, biotech inequality, and the environmental costs of AI.

Portfolio III:

Algorithmic Equity and Future Tech
